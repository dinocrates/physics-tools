    Build compound units with *, / and ^ — e.g. g/cm^3, km/hr, ft^3. Full words work too (miles, hours), and mph/mps/kph/fps are shortcuts. One unit per quantity type per side; liter-based volumes (L, gal, cup…) don’t yet bridge to cubic-length units (cm³, ft³).
