1-D Motion Lab

Two cars pass a fixed reference pipe and drive off down the track. Give each one constant velocity, constant acceleration, or a speed-up → cruise → slow-down trip, offset their start times, and watch position, velocity, and acceleration build up in three synced graphs below. Toggle the area-under-the-curve and tangent-line overlays to connect each graph to the one above and below it.
